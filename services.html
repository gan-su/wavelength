Shopify content automation

Connect your store and the engine reads each product's title, images, pricing, and variants. From that it produces on-brand marketing content for every product, so your catalog stays covered as it grows and changes.

Currently in testing. We are refining output quality before offering it more widely.

What it produces

  • Product imagery

    Styled product scenes from your existing photos and listing details, sized for feeds, ads, and product pages.

  • Short-form video

    Vertical clips built around each product's price, variants, and story.

  • Social copy

    Captions, hooks, and post copy that match your brand voice, generated alongside every asset.
