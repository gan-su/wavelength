Questions we get before the first booking.

How fast can a campaign go live?

Four working days from signed brief to publish, assuming creative is ready. Newsletter slots run on the Thursday cycle, so a Monday brief makes that week.

Do you work with pre-revenue startups?

Yes. Directory listings and banner blocks are priced for teams before a Series A. If the fit is wrong for your stage we will say so rather than sell you a slot.

What reporting do we actually get?

Impressions, unique opens, clicks, referrer breakdown and directory page visits, in a shared dashboard the morning after each send. Raw logs are available on request.

Will our ad run next to a competitor?

Not in the same issue. Newsletter features are one per send and banner blocks cap at three advertisers with no category overlap.

Can you write the creative for us?

Included on featured listings and sponsorships. Our editor drafts it from a call and your site, and nothing ships until you approve the exact wording.

Tell us who you need in the room.

Send the company and the stage. We will reply with the lists that fit, the ones that do not, and the next open date.
