In development

One product first: a content engine for Shopify catalogs.

It reads what is already in your store: product titles, images, pricing, and variants. From that it produces on-brand images, short-form video, and social copy for each product. We are building and testing it now, before offering it more widely.

How it works

Connect your catalog

Serfis reads product titles, images, pricing, variants, and tags directly from your Shopify store. No manual entry.

Generate on-brand content

The engine produces images, short-form video, and captions for each product, matched to your brand's look and voice.

Publish where your customers are

Export ready-to-post assets for your social channels and ad campaigns, and keep pace as your catalog changes.

Where this is going

If the engine performs, it becomes a subscription.

Serfis plans to offer AI content creation as a monthly subscription to businesses that sell physical products. That depends on how the engine performs in testing, so today it is a plan, not an offer.

  • NextTesting on live Shopify catalogs
