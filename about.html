About Serfis

A small company building AI-generated video ads that help funded startups build brand awareness.

Why we exist

Most funded startups know their story. Producing video ads that tell it well, consistently, across channels, is the part that does not scale with a small team.

Serfis exists to take that off your plate, with an AI video engine that turns a few details about your company into a ready-to-post ad.

Building and testing

Early, and honest about it.

We are building and testing our first product, an AI video engine for funded startups. We are not selling it yet. When it performs the way we expect, we plan to offer it as a monthly subscription.

How we work

Honest about what is ready

We label work in development while it is in development, and we do not sell what we have not tested.

Built on your brand

Your company, your industry, and what you're building shape every ad. Our workflows start there instead of asking you to start over.

Your brand, not a template

Generated content should look and sound like you, so brand voice and visual style are inputs, not afterthoughts.
