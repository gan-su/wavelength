About Serfis

A small company building AI workflows for businesses that sell physical products.

Why we exist

Most businesses that sell physical products know what they want to say about them. Producing the images, video, and social posts to say it, for every product, every week, is the part that does not scale.

Serfis exists to take that work off your plate, with workflows that run on the product information your store already has.

Building and testing

Early, and honest about it.

We are building and testing our first product, a content engine for Shopify catalogs. We are not selling it yet. When it performs the way we expect, we plan to offer it as a monthly subscription to businesses that sell physical products.

How we work

Honest about what is ready

We label work in development while it is in development, and we do not sell what we have not tested.

Built on your product data

Your catalog already holds the titles, images, prices, and variants. Our workflows start there instead of asking you to start over.

Your brand, not a template

Generated content should look and sound like you, so brand voice and visual style are inputs, not afterthoughts.
