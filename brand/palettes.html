Palette board

Three directions for Wavelength, shown on the same UI fragment so the comparison is fair. Look at these full screen, in a dark room, on the screen you actually use. Colour on a dark ground behaves nothing like colour in a spec table.

Before you vote: name the emotion you want first, then pick the palette that produces it. Reasoning for each option is in 03-visual-direction.md.

C. Indigo Instrument

Infrastructure. Indigo is the colour of things that handle money, so it buys the credibility violet has to earn. Also the safest of the three: a strong second choice and a weak first one, unless trust turns out to be the main objection.
