# Discovery: ten questions, and the survey to send Pranav

Two parts. **Part A** is the interview two of you should run and answer yourselves first,
so you arrive at the survey with positions rather than blanks. **Part B** is the survey to
send Pranav, written so it can be answered in about eight minutes on a phone.

Rule for Part A: answer in writing before you compare. If two of you write the same answer
independently, it is settled. If you write different answers, that question goes to Pranav.

---

## Part A: the ten questions

**1. Which company is this brand for?**
The notes contain both an ad-space marketplace for startups and Serfis, a marketing company
whose live product serves car dealerships. These need different sites. Answer this first;
the other nine depend on it.

**2. Finish this sentence in one line, no adjectives: "We sell ___ to ___ so they can ___."**
If it takes more than one line, the offer is not defined yet.

**3. Who is the single person who says yes?**
Not a segment. One job title, one company size, one budget range. Name a real person you
have spoken to if you can.

**4. What do they do today instead of buying from us?**
Our real competitor is almost never another ad platform. It is paid social, cold email, or
nothing. Whatever it is, the site has to argue against *that*.

**5. What is the most specific true number we can publish?**
Readers, open rate, click-through, a customer result. If the honest answer is "we do not
have one yet", that is the single most important thing to fix before launch, ahead of any
design work.

**6. What makes a founder hesitate right before paying?**
List the top three objections. Each one earns a line of copy or an FAQ entry. If we cannot
name them, we have not talked to enough customers.

**7. What are we deliberately bad at?**
A brand with no edges has no shape. "We do not do performance marketing." "We will not sell
you a slot if your stage is wrong." Pick something real that we will actually turn down.

**8. Which three brands should we feel adjacent to, and which one should we never be mistaken for?**
Links, not adjectives. The negative example is usually more useful than the positive ones.

**9. What is the one action the site must produce?**
Email, booking form, calendar link, or checkout. One. Everything else on the page is
secondary and should look secondary.

**10. What already exists that we must not throw away?**
Logo files, a name we are committed to, a colour someone has already printed on something,
a domain, an existing customer's expectations.

## Part B: survey for Pranav

> Copy into Google Forms. Question types are marked. Keep it to one page. The whole point is
> that he can finish it without scheduling a call.
>
> **Intro text to paste at the top:**
> *"We are locking the brand direction this week. Eight minutes of your answers now saves us
> a fortnight of guessing. Where you do not have a firm answer, say so; 'not decided yet' is
> a real and useful answer."*

**Section 1: what we are**

1. Which of these is this brand? *(Multiple choice, required)*
   - The startup ad-space marketplace
   - Serfis (marketing and content for businesses)
   - Both, under one parent brand
   - Not decided yet

2. In one sentence and no adjectives, what do we sell and to whom? *(Short answer, required)*

3. Are we committed to the name? *(Multiple choice)*
   - Yes, locked
   - Working name, open to changing it
   - Actively want a new name
   *(Follow-up, short answer: if it is locked, who decided, and is the domain owned?)*

**Section 2: who buys**

4. Describe the last person who paid us, or the closest we have come. *(Paragraph)*
   Role, company size, what they bought, what made them say yes.

5. What is the realistic budget range for a first purchase? *(Multiple choice)*
   Under $500 / $500 to $2,000 / $2,000 to $10,000 / Over $10,000 / Not decided yet

6. What do these buyers spend this money on today if not on us? *(Short answer)*

**Section 3: proof**

7. Which of these can we put on the site **right now, truthfully**? *(Checkboxes, select all)*
   - Audience size
   - Open or click-through rate
   - A named customer
   - A customer result with a number
   - Company logos we have written permission to use
   - A screenshot of our own product
   - None of these yet

8. For anything ticked above, paste the real figures or say where to find them. *(Paragraph)*
   *Note: the current page uses placeholder numbers. They are marked in the code and must be
   replaced or removed before this is public.*

**Section 4: feel**

9. Pick up to three words the brand should feel like. *(Checkboxes, max 3)*
   Precise / Warm / Serious / Playful / Expensive / Accessible / Technical / Editorial /
   Fast / Established

10. Paste two or three links to sites whose feel you like, and one whose feel you dislike.
    *(Paragraph)* The dislike is the most useful answer on this form.

11. Any colour, typeface or logo we are already committed to? *(Short answer)*
    Anything printed, filed, or already shown to a customer.

**Section 5: the ask**

12. What is the single action the site should produce? *(Multiple choice)*
    Email us / Book a call / Fill a form / Pay directly / Join a waitlist

13. Anything above that you disagree with, or that we have framed wrong? *(Paragraph)*

## What to do with the answers

- Q1 and Q12 unblock the copy. Nothing final gets written before they land.
- Q7 and Q8 decide whether we can launch at all. No real proof means the social-proof
  section becomes a "what we are building" section instead of a fabricated one.
- Q9 and Q10 settle the palette vote in `03-visual-direction.md`. Run the vote after this
  form comes back, not before.
